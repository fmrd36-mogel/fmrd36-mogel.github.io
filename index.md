# AI 한입 (AI Hanip)

Korean YouTube channel that explains the latest AI news, AI tool features and how-to guides in short, easy videos.

최신 AI 뉴스, AI 스킬·기능 소개, AI 도구 연결 방법을 매일 쉽고 정확하게 정리하는 유튜브 채널입니다. 모든 영상 설명란에는 원문 출처 링크를 함께 답니다.

## AI Hanip Uploader

AI Hanip Uploader is the channel owner's private desktop tool. It uses **YouTube API Services** to upload the owner's videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel, organize playlists, read channel statistics and analytics, and help answer viewer comments. It is not offered to other users.
